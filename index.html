FAQ

Can I play GTA 6 on PC with the leaked files?
Not yet. The EXE is an Xbox Series X binary — Windows doesn't speak its system calls. Dropping it into the 209 GB asset folder does nothing. Open-source recomp projects are working on a native PC port; watch gta6recomp.com for progress.
What exactly do the downloads contain?
The ~209 GB torrent is the decrypted Xbox build's assets (Nov 2026, v1.0.2.1). The small ZIP is the main executable missing from it. Together: the complete console build, for archival and reverse-engineering.
Is it safe to download?
Only if the hashes match — that's why Cyberleek published them. Verify the ZIP SHA-256 before extracting and the EXE SHA-256 after. Fake sites and Discord/Telegram "launchers" are the real danger.
Who is Cyberleek?
An anonymous leaker dribbling GTA VI material since before launch. All releases free; takes crypto donations. Official site: cyberleek.org.
