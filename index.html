❓ FAQ

Can I play GTA 6 on PC with this?
Not yet. The EXE is an Xbox Series X binary — Windows doesn't speak its system calls. Dropping it into the 209 GB asset folder does nothing. Open-source recomp projects (Leonida etc.) are working on a native PC port; a working recomp is expected to need 350 GB+ of drive space. Watch gta6recomp.com for progress.
What exactly do I get?
The ~209 GB torrent is the decrypted Xbox build's assets (Nov 2026, v1.0.2.1). The small ZIP contains the main executable that was missing from it. Together: the complete console build, for archival and reverse-engineering.
Is it safe to download?
Only if the hashes match — that's exactly why Cyberleek published them. Verify the ZIP SHA-256 before extracting, and the EXE SHA-256 after. Fake sites and Discord/Telegram "launchers" are the real danger.
Who is Cyberleek?
An anonymous leaker who's been dribbling GTA VI material since before launch. All releases are free; they take crypto donations (BTC/XMR) and shill a CYBERLEEK token. Official site: cyberleek.org.
Is this "official"?
Official GTA 6 = Rockstar Games, PS5 / Xbox Series X|S only. This is a leaked console build and this page is a fan-made community archive — not affiliated with Rockstar or Cyberleek.
